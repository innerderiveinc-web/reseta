❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About RESETA™

Clear, transparent answers on employee privacy, clinical boundaries, sound therapy, and enterprise deployment.

🔒 Privacy & Employee Confidentiality

Can my manager, HR, or CEO see my personal check-ins or reflections?
No, absolutely never. RESETA™ was engineered under a strict zero-surveillance architectural guarantee. There is no API or database view that allows an employer or manager to view an individual contributor's stress levels, input text, or reset logs. Managers only see anonymized, aggregate squad themes when at least 5 people share similar signals (k-anonymity ≥ 5).
How does RESETA™ comply with the Indian DPDP Act, 2023?
RESETA™ is fully compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. We collect explicit, purpose-limited consent, store all data strictly in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1), and provide built-in 1-click tools for employees to exercise their statutory rights: Right to Access Summary (Export) and Right to Erasure (Permanent Purge).
Are voice transcripts or microphone audio recorded?
No. When you use "Talk It Out™", speech-to-text processing occurs directly via browser speech synthesis or temporary encrypted memory. Raw audio is never stored on disk or used for corporate surveillance.

🩺 Clinical Scope & Medical Disclaimers

Is RESETA™ a replacement for a psychologist, psychiatrist, or medical therapy?
No. RESETA™ is a preventative, non-clinical cognitive offloading and micro-recovery platform designed for workplace friction. It does not diagnose mental illness or prescribe medical treatment. If an employee reports severe crisis, the system automatically bypasses workplace protocols and connects them to 24/7 helplines like the Govt. of India Tele-MANAS (14416).
Who authored and reviewed the reset protocols?
All protocols in the RESETA™ catalog (Box Breathing, Somatic Sigh, Mental Load Unload™, Cognitive De-escalation) were reviewed by qualified clinical psychologists and behavioural researchers adhering to evidence-based protocols (CBT, somatic grounding, and attention restoration).

🎵 Continuous Soundscapes & Voice Guidance

Why does continuous sound play during a reset?
Silence during stress exercises often causes the mind to wander back to work deadlines. Our Web Audio engine synthesizes warm, continuous 432 Hz Solfeggio tones with an 8-second breathing rhythm (0.125 Hz) that physically guides your respiratory rate into heart rate variability (HRV) coherence.
How does the "Eyes-Closed Mode" work?
When a reset starts, our Voice Coach speaks instructions aloud at a calm, soothing pace. A countdown timer automatically advances to the next step when ready, accompanied by a soft chime. You can close your eyes throughout the entire 2 to 5 minutes without needing to touch your screen.

⚙️ Enterprise Deployment & Integrations

How does RESETA™ integrate with Slack and Microsoft Teams?
Employees can simply type /reset in any Slack or Teams direct message window. An ephemeral card immediately appears offering a 2-minute, 5-minute, or 10-minute quiet reset. The card is visible only to that user and generates no public channel noise.
How long does an enterprise rollout take?
Typically under 48 hours. Using standard SCIM 2.0 automated provisioning and SAML 2.0 SSO (Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace), user accounts are synchronized automatically without manual CSV uploads.
