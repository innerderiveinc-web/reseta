RESETA™ Enterprise Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Version: 2026.1-DPDP · Governing Law: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)
The Zero-Surveillance Core Guarantee:
RESETA™ is designed such that your employer, human resources department, or direct manager cannot see your individual check-ins, stress ratings, or written reflections. Privacy is enforced cryptographically and architecturally.

1. Identity of the Data Fiduciary

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the primary Data Fiduciary for the technical operation of the RESETA™ platform is Qsura India Private Limited ("Qsura India", "we", "us", or "our"), having its registered corporate presence in Bengaluru and New Delhi, India.

2. Categories of Personal Data We Process

We strictly limit data collection to what is essential for the provision of employee micro-recovery:

  • Account & Identity Data: Professional workplace email address, employee full name, organization tenant ID, department ID, and team ID (synchronized automatically via SCIM 2.0 or enterprise SSO).
  • Self-Reported Wellness Signals: Current self-selected stress score (1-10), self-selected state category (e.g. Overloaded, Fast Breathing, Racing Mind), and protocol completion metrics.
  • Technical Session Metadata: Timestamp of session, protocol duration (2, 5, or 10 min), and client telemetry for SLA maintenance.

3. Categories of Data We STRICTLY DO NOT Collect

To preserve uncompromised psychological safety, the RESETA™ platform has architectural blockades against collecting:

  • No Audio or Voice Storage: Raw microphone input during "Talk It Out™" is processed ephemerally in browser memory and immediately discarded. No voice files are saved to any server.
  • No Keystroke or Activity Tracking: We do not track employee screen activity, mouse clicks, idle time, or background tabs.
  • No Medical Diagnosis or Prescription Records: RESETA™ is a preventative cognitive offloader, not a medical clinic. We hold zero medical history or psychiatric records.

4. Purpose of Processing & Consent Mechanism

In accordance with Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, data is processed solely on the ground of freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent:

  • Purpose A: Immediate Wellness Delivery: Generating your customized breathing, somatic, or cognitive offloading sequence.
  • Purpose B: Personal Mind Mirror™: Allowing you to view your own historical stress patterns over time (visible only to you).
  • Purpose C: Differential Privacy Aggregation: Calculating departmental averages under a strict mathematical threshold of k ≥ 5 (if fewer than 5 members in a squad use the tool, reports are suppressed to prevent re-identification).

5. Data Sovereignty & Localization

Pursuant to MeitY guidelines and Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act, all production database clusters, encrypted backups, and vector models reside entirely within the sovereign territory of the Republic of India at Amazon Web Services (AWS) ap-south-1 (Mumbai Region). Zero sensitive personal data is transferred across international borders.

6. Statutory Rights of the Data Principal

Every employee using RESETA™ possesses clear, unhindered statutory rights under Chapter III of the DPDP Act:

  • Right to Access Summary (Section 11): You may at any time click "DPDP Data Export" in your settings to immediately download a complete, readable archive of your personal wellness data.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): You may request the immediate, permanent purge of your historical wellness data via the in-app "Request Data Erasure" trigger. Your records are purged within 72 hours.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): You have the right to readily accessible grievance redressal provided by Qsura India Pvt. Ltd.
