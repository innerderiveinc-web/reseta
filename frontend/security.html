🚫 Strict Zero-Surveillance Architectural Guarantee

The #1 reason workplace mental health platforms fail is employee fear of being judged, tagged as "low resilience", or retaliated against in appraisals. RESETA™ eliminates this at the cryptographic and database layer:

  • No Manager Visibility: Managers cannot view individual employee stress ratings, check-ins, or reflections under any circumstances.
  • Strict k ≥ 5 Cohort Suppression: Departmental wellbeing themes are only visible when at least 5 contributors share a similar aggregate pattern. If a team has fewer than 5 members, reporting is automatically suppressed.
  • No Surveillance Webhooks: Integrations with Slack and Microsoft Teams use ephemeral message cards visible solely to the individual user. Nothing is posted to public channels.
