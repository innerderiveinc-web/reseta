RESETA Anti-Surveillance & Privacy Charter

Our legally binding architectural boundary with employers
1. Zero Individual Employee Visibility to Managers

Managers can never see personal stress ratings, check-ins, or thought unloads. Only anonymous aggregate squad themes ($k \ge 5$) are ever surfaced.

2. Absolute Zero Productivity Surveillance

RESETA contains zero keystroke loggers, screen captures, calendar surveillance, or microphone eavesdropping. Contextual autopilot triggers are purely local and opt-in.

3. Strict Non-Diagnostic Clinical Firewall

RESETA never issues psychiatric diagnoses (e.g. depression, anxiety disorder, ADHD). Any acute distress triggers immediate, confidential human crisis referral.

4. Zero Model Retraining Policy

Employee personal inputs are never ingested into machine learning training datasets. Conversations are ephemeral and encrypted at rest.
